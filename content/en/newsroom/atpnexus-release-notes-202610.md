# ATP Nexus adds new integrations beyond Bluesky and powerful new features
**From articles and live streams to communities and events, the world of the AT Protocol comes together in one app — now with support for iPhone Duo and Apple Intelligence**

![The home screen of ATP Nexus](/images/newsroom/atpnexus-202610-home.webp "The home screen of ATP Nexus")

October 28, 2026 — Today, the latest update to ATP Nexus, the Bluesky client for Apple devices, was announced. With this update, the app steps outside the world of Bluesky and integrates with a range of services on the AT Protocol, including blogs, live streams, communities and events. It also brings new features that make the most of Apple devices, including split view designed for iPhone Duo, thread explanations and alt text generation with Apple Intelligence, and expanded actions for Siri and Shortcuts.

## The world of the AT Protocol, in one app
Beyond Bluesky, a growing number of services are being built on the AT Protocol, from blogs and live streams to communities and events. With new integrations in ATP Nexus Pro, you can enjoy these experiences right inside the app.

Articles from standard.site-based services such as WhiteWind, Leaflet, pckt.blog and Offprint can be read without switching apps. Live streams and videos from Streamplace can be watched together with their live chat. On Frontpage, you can browse topics and post comments, and events on atmo.rsvp can be viewed right from the app. Read, watch, talk and gather — all in one app.

Available to all users, the app now also displays and edits Linkat links. And with the new Extended Services setting, each integrated external service can be turned on or off individually, so you can choose only the services you want and make ATP Nexus your own.

## Split view designed for iPhone Duo
The app now supports split view on iPhone Duo. Make full use of the expansive display and enjoy Bluesky with greater comfort than ever before.

![The profile screen of ATP Nexus](/images/newsroom/atpnexus-202610-profile.webp "The profile screen of ATP Nexus")

## Conversations made clearer with Apple Intelligence
Even long threads are clearly explained by Apple Intelligence, helping you quickly follow the flow of a conversation. Alt text for images can also be generated automatically, making it easier than ever to create posts that reach everyone.

## Deeper integration with Apple devices
More actions are now available in Siri and Shortcuts, including sending DMs and opening each screen. Apple Music playback can be controlled from the Lock Screen and Control Center. The extra large vertical widget is now also available on iOS and macOS.

## A more comfortable experience
Swipe actions are now available for posts, accounts and the chat list. Posts longer than 300 characters are automatically split into a thread, so you can post without worrying about the character limit. Starter pack search and a setting to ask not to be shown in recommendation algorithms have also been added.

## Pricing and availability
- The latest update to ATP Nexus is available today on the App Store.
- It supports iOS 27.0, iPadOS 27.0, visionOS 27.0, macOS 27.0 and watchOS 26.1 or later.
- Integrations with standard.site-based articles, Streamplace, Frontpage and atmo.rsvp are available with ATP Nexus Pro, a paid in-app plan.
- Video captions are temporarily unavailable.
- Attaching GIFs requires enabling KLIPY in Settings.

## About ATP Nexus
A client app for Bluesky, the social network built on the AT Protocol. Enjoy your feeds and lists freely, with features optimized for Apple devices such as multiple accounts, Handoff and widgets.

Thank you for your continued support of ATP Nexus.
